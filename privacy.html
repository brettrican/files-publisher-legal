THE FILES Publisher

Privacy policy

Effective October 26, 2026

Who uses this app

THE FILES Publisher is used only by its owner, to publish videos to the owner's own channels and accounts. It is not offered to the public, and it does not collect information about viewers of those videos.

What it accesses

When the owner connects an account, the app receives access only to that account, through each platform's official sign-in:

  • YouTube (YouTube API Services): uploading videos to the connected channel, setting their visibility and publish time, and reading the channel name, subscriber count and the view, like and comment counts of videos it published.
  • TikTok: sending videos to the connected account, and reading its display name, follower count and the statistics of videos it published.
  • Instagram and Facebook (Meta): publishing Reels to the connected Instagram account and Facebook Page, and reading their follower counts and the insights of posts it published.

What it stores, and where

It stores the sign-in tokens for the connected accounts, the account names, the videos it scheduled, and the statistics listed above. Everything is kept on a private server controlled by the owner. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or sent to analytics services.

Google user data

THE FILES Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and data

Access can be revoked at any time: for Google, at Google account connections; for TikTok and Meta, in each app's settings under connected or business integrations. When an account is disconnected, its stored tokens are deleted from the server, and its statistics are deleted on request.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new effective date. Questions can be sent to the developer contact shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
